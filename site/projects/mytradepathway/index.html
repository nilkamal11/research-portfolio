MyTradePathway

A browser-only career-pathway demo with student, counselor, and administrator journeys, practice activities, and local progress.

Functional synthetic demo

Useful for Career education teams and product reviewers

The question

How could a student move from exploring trades to choosing a pathway and preparing for next steps?

My contribution

Built representative journeys for trade exploration, preparation, practice, counselor reporting, and sample content review, using synthetic accounts and browser-local state.

Data & approach

The public demo includes ten trades, twelve supplied local program records, and 135 original practice questions. Synthetic student and activity fixtures support role-based demonstrations; progress stays in the current browser.

What it delivers

A functional product demonstration with locally generated reports and an offline public demo shell.

Interpretation & boundaries

Roles simulate access; there is no live authentication, payment, shared state, or server API. People and activity are synthetic. Supplied wages, fees, eligibility, and deadlines are unverified, and some windows have closed. It demonstrates a product concept rather than measured student outcomes.
