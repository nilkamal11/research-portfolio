Featured project · Census F-33 and SEDA

North Palos district finance

Explore FY2022–2024 spending for North Palos 117 and three nearby elementary districts. The view separates current spending from capital outlay and shows district-wide spending alongside fourth-grade SEDA estimates.

I joined year-specific district identities, converted source dollar units and built 96 validation checks. The checks exposed missing records in an official CSV and differences between gross and published per-pupil measures. Those issues are documented, not hidden.

Education finance · SED 2024

Doctoral borrowing

Compare doctoral debt and financial support across fields and racial and ethnic groups. I reconciled three NCSES tables and their response denominators in Python before building the D3 views.

Published aggregates do not link each person's funding source to their debt. The views preserve that limitation and unavailable values.

Explore doctoral fields, employment sectors and primary work activities. I built the survey-weighted cross-tab from public-use records and kept the separate published salary comparison tied to its own population.

About the work

Make the data usable without losing its meaning

My professional experience spans higher-education research analytics, university data integration and K–12 research and reporting. It includes Python and SQL pipelines, Tableau and Power BI dashboards, financial-aid reporting and department budget-tracking tools.

These independent portfolio projects make that approach visible: identify the population and reporting level, preserve original sources, check joins and totals, and explain what the results do and do not support.

Portfolio organized October 24, 2026. Individual sources retain their own release and review dates.
