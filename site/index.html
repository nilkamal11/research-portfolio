Doctorate careers

Private for-profit employers accounted for 61.1% of working engineering doctorate holders in the U.S. in 2023; educational institutions employed 63.3% of those in the social sciences.

I built the weighted three-way cross-tab behind these views, preserving the field, employer and activity recorded for each respondent.

Student borrowing over time

Indebted Over Time: Racial Differences in Student Borrowing

Educational Researcher, 2019, 48(8), 558–563.

I was the data person on this research team and a coauthor of the paper. I built the analysis datasets from five waves of the National Postsecondary Student Aid Study (NPSAS), then brought the results back to the team for discussion.

Race categories and other variables changed between rounds, so comparing borrowing over time meant working out which definitions lined up and recoding the data consistently. The doctoral borrowing project brought me back to that kind of work: checking who a table actually counts before comparing the numbers.
